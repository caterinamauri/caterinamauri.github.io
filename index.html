Caterina Mauri

I study how the grammars of the world’s languages take shape in use, as speakers interact, negotiate knowledge and build meaning together.

I use this website to bring together the different strands of my work—research, publications, linguistic resources, collaborations, teaching, and public engagement—and provide a shared point of access to them.

Research approach

Following questions where they lead

I do not think of research as following a route fixed in advance. I prefer to follow a promising detail and see which paths it opens. Curiosity may lead from a connective to cognition, from a conversational fragment to typology, or from a corpus problem to a new way of sharing data.

The direction is not arbitrary: linguistic diversity, change and interaction are the landscape in which I keep moving. I work through converging evidence, bringing cross-linguistic comparison, spoken corpora, discourse analysis, diachrony and experimental methods into dialogue.
