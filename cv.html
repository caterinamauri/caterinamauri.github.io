I am Associate Professor of Linguistics at the University of Bologna. My work moves across linguistic typology, language change, interaction and the construction of open spoken-language resources.

The pages of this website offer a selected, navigable account of my work. The full curriculum contains the complete chronology of academic positions, visiting periods, funded projects, teaching, supervision, editorial roles, institutional responsibilities, invited talks and publications.
