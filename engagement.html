01

Listening to diversity

European Researchers’ Night · 27.09.2024

Senti KIParla!

An interactive experience in Piazza Scaravilli built around audio from the KIParla corpus. Through games, quizzes and listening activities, participants explored regional and social variation in Italian and questioned stereotypes such as the idea of a single native-speaker norm.

With Eleonora Zucchini, Ludovica Pannitto and Claudia Borghetti.
