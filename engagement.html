01

Listening to diversity

European Researchers’ Night · 27.09.2024

Senti KIParla!

An interactive experience in Piazza Scaravilli built around audio from the KIParla corpus. Through games, quizzes and listening activities, participants explored regional and social variation in Italian and questioned stereotypes such as the idea of a single native-speaker norm.

With Eleonora Zucchini, Ludovica Pannitto and Claudia Borghetti.

02

Podcast

Parole in transito · 22.05.2026

Episode 1 — Appartenenza

What makes us feel that we belong to a place? Developed from the work of the LILEC workshop AttraVerso la mia città, this episode follows emotional maps, life stories and the everyday paths of migrant communities through Bologna, exploring the connections between language, emotions and urban space.

A podcast produced by the University of Bologna within the TransformAzioni public-engagement project.
