Resources

I develop open, modular and reusable linguistic resources as shared research infrastructures.

They are designed to grow over time, document variation rather than erase it, and keep data, documentation and annotation choices open to scrutiny.

KIParla

I co-founded and co-coordinate KIParla with Silvia Ballarè, Ludovica Pannitto, Massimo Cerruti and Eugenio Goria. I am co-author of KIP, KIPasti, ParlaBO and Stra-ParlaBO.
