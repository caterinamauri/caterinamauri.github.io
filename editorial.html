Journal leadership · 2021—

Linguistic Typology at the Crossroads

Founder and Editor-in-Chief

I founded and direct LTC as an international diamond open-access journal for research at the intersections between linguistic typology and diachrony, sociolinguistics, psycholinguistics, corpus research, speech and discourse. The journal is rated Class A by ANVUR, indexed in ERIH PLUS and part of LingOA.
