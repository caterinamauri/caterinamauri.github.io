Research

My research explores linguistic diversity across languages, across time and within communities of speakers.

I am especially interested in the ways people build categories, connect ideas and negotiate meaning in real interaction.

I combine cross-linguistic comparison with corpus-based discourse analysis, diachronic investigation and psycholinguistic methods: different kinds of evidence for a more complete account of language.
